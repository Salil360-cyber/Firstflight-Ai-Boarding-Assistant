# SaffronSky Air: Passenger Policy Handbook (v1.0)

> **Fictional airline.** SaffronSky Air does not exist. This handbook was written for the FirstFlight
> portfolio project so the AI assistant can be tested against a fixed, known set of rules.
> None of these rules describe any real airline or any regulator's requirements.

SaffronSky Air is a low-cost airline based in Pune, flying domestic routes in India and
international routes to the Gulf. All prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and are per passenger, per flight.

---

## P1. Fare types

| Fare | Cabin bag | Checked bag included | Change before departure | Cancellation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lite** | Yes | None | Not allowed | Only airport taxes refunded |
| **Value** | Yes | 20 kg | Fee + fare difference | Fee deducted, rest refunded |
| **Flex** | Yes | 30 kg | Free (pay fare difference only) | ₹1,000 deducted, rest refunded |

- **P1.1** Change fee on Value fares: ₹1,500 domestic, ₹3,000 international, plus any fare difference.
- **P1.2** Cancellation fee on Value fares: ₹3,000 domestic, ₹5,000 international.
- **P1.3** Changes and cancellations can be made in Manage Booking up to **4 hours** before departure.
- **P1.4** Seat selection is free on Flex fares.

## P2. Baggage

- **P2.1 Cabin bag:** 1 bag, up to **7 kg**, maximum size 55 × 40 × 20 cm, plus **1 personal item** up to **3 kg** (handbag or laptop bag).
- **P2.2 Checked bag add-ons bought online (domestic):** 15 kg ₹1,800 · 20 kg ₹2,400 · 30 kg ₹3,600.
- **P2.3 Checked bag add-ons bought online (international):** 20 kg ₹3,200 · 30 kg ₹4,500.
- **P2.4 Extra weight at the airport:** ₹600 per kg domestic, ₹1,000 per kg international. Buying online before the flight is cheaper.
- **P2.5** Online baggage add-ons can be bought up to **4 hours** before departure.
- **P2.6** A single checked bag cannot weigh more than **32 kg**.
- **P2.7 Power banks:** cabin bag only, never in checked bags; up to **100 Wh** each; maximum **2** per passenger.
- **P2.8 Liquids on international flights:** in the cabin, containers of up to **100 ml** each, all in one clear 1-litre bag.

## P3. Check-in

| | Domestic | International |
|---|---|---|
| Web check-in opens | 48 hours before | 48 hours before |
| Web check-in closes | 60 minutes before | 3 hours before |
| Airport counter closes | 45 minutes before | 60 minutes before |

- **P3.1** Web check-in is free for all fares.
- **P3.2** International passengers must visit the airport counter for a document check even after web check-in.
- **P3.3** After web check-in, the boarding pass can be downloaded or sent to WhatsApp or email. A printout is not required on domestic flights.

## P4. Boarding

| | Domestic | International |
|---|---|---|
| Boarding starts | 45 minutes before | 60 minutes before |
| Gate closes | 20 minutes before | 30 minutes before |

- **P4.1** Passengers who reach the gate after it closes cannot board and are treated as a no-show (no refund on Lite fares).

## P5. Travel documents

- **P5.1 Domestic:** an original government photo ID: Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence.
- **P5.2 International:** a passport valid for at least **6 months** after the travel date, plus a valid visa or entry permit for the destination.
- **P5.3** SaffronSky Air does not issue visas. Visa questions go to the destination country's official visa service.

## P6. Infants and children

- **P6.1** An infant is a child under **2 years** on the travel date. Infants travel on an adult's lap.
- **P6.2** Infant fee: ₹1,500 domestic, ₹4,000 international. One infant per adult.
- **P6.3** One stroller per infant is carried free in the hold.
- **P6.4** Children under **12** cannot travel alone. Passengers aged 12–17 can travel alone with a signed parental consent form.

## P7. Special assistance

- **P7.1** Wheelchair assistance is free. Request it at least **48 hours** before departure in Manage Booking.
- **P7.2** Requests made later are handled on a best-effort basis at the airport.

## P8. Add-ons

- **P8.1 Seats:** standard seats ₹250–₹800; extra-legroom seats ₹1,200. Free on Flex fares.
- **P8.2 Meals:** pre-book up to **24 hours** before departure, ₹350–₹550. Snacks can also be bought on board.

## P9. Name corrections

- **P9.1** Spelling corrections of up to **3 characters** are free through the contact centre, up to 24 hours before departure.
- **P9.2** A booking cannot be transferred to a different person.

## P10. Delays and cancellations by the airline

- **P10.1** Delay of more than **3 hours:** a free meal voucher at the airport.
- **P10.2** Delay of more than **6 hours**, or a cancellation by the airline: free change to the next available SaffronSky Air flight, or a full refund.
- **P10.3** Refunds go back to the original payment method within **7 working days**.
- **P10.4** Live flight status is shown on the website and app. The assistant cannot see live flight status.

## P11. What the assistant must not do

- **P11.1** Never ask for or store passport numbers, Aadhaar numbers, card numbers or OTPs.
- **P11.2** It cannot look up, change or cancel a specific booking. Booking-specific requests go to an agent.
- **P11.3** If the answer is not in this handbook, say so and offer an agent. Do not guess.
